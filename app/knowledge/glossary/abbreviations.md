## Document Number Format

```
<number>/<year>/<issuer-code>
```

Examples:
- `22/2023/QH15` — Resolution 22 of year 2023, National Assembly session 15
- `214/2025/NĐ-CP` — Decree 214 of year 2025, by the Government (Chính phủ)
- `104/2026/NĐ-CP` — Decree 104 of year 2026, by the Government
- `79/2025/TT-BTC` — Circular 79 of year 2025, Ministry of Finance
- `13/2026/TT-BCT` — Circular 13 of year 2026, Ministry of Industry and Trade

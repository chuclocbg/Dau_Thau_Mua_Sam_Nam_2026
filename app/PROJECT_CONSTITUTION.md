# PROJECT CONSTITUTION
## Nền tảng Quản lý Đấu thầu Mua sắm — Platform Engineering Principles

Version: 1.0
Status: FROZEN
Effective: 2026-07-02

This document defines immutable platform principles.
Principles may only be superseded by a new numbered version, not modified in place.

---

## PRINCIPLE 1 — Legal Engine is the only legal reasoning component

All procurement method selection, approval authority resolution, threshold determination,
and legal document identification flow through ProcurementEngine.evaluate().

No module may re-implement Vietnamese procurement law logic independently.

New decrees supersede rule definitions in procurementRules.ts — they do not justify
a second reasoning engine elsewhere in the platform.

---

## PRINCIPLE 2 — Workflow owns state

WorkflowInstance is the single source of truth for the procurement lifecycle state.

State transitions are performed exclusively through workflowEngine.advance().
No module may write directly to WorkflowInstance.currentState.
No module may bypass the workflow validator to force a state change.

---

## PRINCIPLE 3 — History is immutable

Approval history, workflow history, audit trails, and decision records are
append-only. Once written, a history entry is never modified or deleted.

This is a legal requirement under Vietnamese public procurement regulations.

---

## PRINCIPLE 4 — Planning precedes Procurement Package

A ProcurementPlan must exist before a ProcurementPackage is created.
A ProcurementRequest must be approved before it can be included in a plan.
A PackageProposal must be approved before it converts into a ProcurementPackage.

No shortcut path from request to package exists.

---

## PRINCIPLE 5 — Documents are generated, never manually authored

All official procurement documents (bidding dossiers, approval decisions, contracts,
acceptance records) are generated by the Document Generator from structured data.

No module may produce a legally-binding document by string concatenation, template
filling outside the Document Generator, or manual authorship by business logic.

---

## PRINCIPLE 6 — Master Data owns every reference value

Department codes, fund source codes, package type codes, procurement method codes,
approval authority codes, and document template codes are stored in and served by
the Master Data module.

No business service may hardcode a reference value that belongs in Master Data.
Every reference code is looked up at runtime from MasterDataRepositories.

---

## PRINCIPLE 7 — AI never contains business logic

AI components (language models, embeddings, classifiers) may assist with text
generation, search, summarization, and recommendation. They must never make
legally-binding procurement decisions.

Every AI output that influences a procurement outcome must be validated by the
ProcurementEngine or a domain service before being persisted.

---

## PRINCIPLE 8 — Every decision must be traceable

Every procurement decision (method selection, approval authority, threshold band,
legal basis, document requirement) carries a LegalBasis struct that names the
specific Vietnamese law document and article it was derived from.

Decisions without traceable legal authority are rejected at the domain boundary.

---

## PRINCIPLE 9 — Law versions are time-aware

Rules are associated with effectiveFrom and effectiveTo dates.
The rule engine evaluates rules as of the asOfDate provided in each ProcurementCase.

When a new decree supersedes an old one:
- New rules are added with the correct effectiveFrom date.
- Old rules are closed with an effectiveTo date.
- No rule is deleted from the system; the history of the law is preserved.

---

## PRINCIPLE 10 — Production correctness is always more important than implementation elegance

When correctness and elegance conflict, correctness wins.
A verbose but correct validation is preferred over a concise but incorrect one.
A slow but legally correct document is preferred over a fast but incomplete one.

This platform governs real public money. Errors have legal consequences.

---

## ENFORCEMENT

These principles are enforced by:
- Architecture Decision Records (docs/adr/)
- Coding standards (.memory/coding-rules.md)
- Code review against known-issues.md

Any implementation that violates a principle must be corrected before merge,
regardless of test coverage or business pressure.

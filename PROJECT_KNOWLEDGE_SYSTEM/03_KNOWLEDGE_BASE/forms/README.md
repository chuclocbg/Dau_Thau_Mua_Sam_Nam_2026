# Forms

**Purpose:** Catalog of official forms used throughout the procurement lifecycle.

**Status:** Scaffolded, not yet populated.

## Future Contents

- Index of every official form (bid forms, guarantee forms, acceptance minutes) by procurement
  stage, with the legal instrument that mandates each form's structure.
- Distinction from [`templates/`](../templates/README.md): a Form is the *official, mandated
  structure*; a Template is *this system's generated document* implementing that structure.
  Do not conflate the two when populating either folder.

## Ownership

Owned jointly by whoever maintains the legal corpus (forms are legally mandated structures)
and `src/procurement/`. Per [`../../KNOWLEDGE_BASE_EDITOR_GUIDE.md`](../../KNOWLEDGE_BASE_EDITOR_GUIDE.md).

## Update Policy

Additive, per [`../../DOCUMENTATION_CHANGE_POLICY.md`](../../DOCUMENTATION_CHANGE_POLICY.md).
Update when a new official form is introduced or an existing one's mandated structure changes.
